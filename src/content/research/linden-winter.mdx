## The conjecture

Linden and Winter proved a constrained four-party entropy inequality.
Their [Conjecture 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0406162) asked whether positive constants $k_1,k_2,k_3$ could make

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_k={}&k_1 I(A;C\mid B)+k_2 I(C;B\mid A)\\
&+k_3 I(A;B\mid D)+I(C;D)-I(C;AB)\geq0
\end{aligned}
$$

hold for all four-party quantum states.
Here $I(X;Y\mid Z)=H(XZ)+H(YZ)-H(Z)-H(XYZ)$ is conditional mutual information.
A classical counterexample is sufficient because classical distributions embed as diagonal quantum states.

## The family

For an integer $L\geq2$, assign the following weights to six bit strings in $(A,B,C,D)$ order, then divide by their sum:

| Atom | Integer weight |
| --- | --- |
| $0000$ | $2\cdot10^{4L+3}$ |
| $0010$ | $27\cdot10^{4L+3}-27\cdot10^{3L+3}$ |
| $1101$ | $2\cdot10^{4L}$ |
| $1111$ | $27\cdot10^{4L}$ |
| $0101$ | $2\cdot10^{L+3}$ |
| $0110$ | $27\cdot10^{L+3}$ |

Two terms vanish exactly.
Given $D=0$, $A$ is constant; given $D=1$, $B$ is constant.
Hence $I(A;B\mid D)=0$.
For $B=0$, $A$ is constant.
For $B=1$, conditional independence of $A$ and $C$ follows from

$$
w(1101)w(0110)=w(1111)w(0101)=54\cdot10^{5L+3}.
$$

Thus $I(A;C\mid B)=0$ too.
Write $y_L=I(C;B\mid A)$ and $e_L=I(C;D)-I(C;AB)$.
On this family, $f_k=k_2y_L+e_L$, independently of $k_1$ and $k_3$.

## Why no finite coefficient works

Set $\delta=10^{-L}$.
The equivalent weights are $2,27(1-\delta),2/1000,27/1000,2\delta^3,27\delta^3$.
Their sum is $Z=29029/1000-27\delta+29\delta^3$.
Define

$$
\Phi(a,b)=(a+b)\ln(a+b)-a\ln a-b\ln b.
$$

Using natural logarithms for this derivation, direct conditional-entropy grouping gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
Zy_L={}&\Phi(2+2\delta^3,27(1-\delta)+27\delta^3)\\
&-\Phi(2,27(1-\delta))-\delta^3\Phi(2,27),\\[3pt]
Ze_L={}&\Phi(2,27(1-\delta))+\Phi(2/1000,27/1000)\\
&+\delta^3\Phi(2,27)-\Phi(2,27(1-\delta)+27\delta^3)\\
&-\Phi(2/1000+2\delta^3,27/1000).
\end{aligned}
$$

Every argument of $\Phi$ in these expressions is positive in a neighborhood of zero, so the expressions are analytic there.
Taylor expansion gives

$$
y_L=\frac{27000}{841841}\delta^5+O(\delta^6),\qquad
 e_L=-\frac{54000}{841841}\delta^4+O(\delta^5).
$$

These coefficients were recomputed symbolically from the displayed expressions during this review.
The [derivation receipt](/research/lw6-asymptotic-receipt.json) records the lower-order cancellations and exact coefficients.
The formulas also permit direct differentiation without trusting that computation.
Consequently,

$$
R_L=-e_L/y_L=2/\delta+O(1)\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

For every finite positive $k_2$, some $L$ satisfies $R_L>k_2$, making $f_k<0$.
This analytic argument carries the universal statement; a finite table cannot establish it on its own.

## Verification and scope

The [public repository](https://github.com/drewstone/lw6-counterexample) contains a written note and a standard-library interval verifier.
The verifier checks structural identities, finite positive and negative entropy signs, ratio bounds for $L=2,\ldots,16$, and explicit coefficient examples.
Its default invocation replayed successfully in the October 2 review.
The advertised arbitrary-coefficient invocation exposed a Python conversion error.
That defect was [fixed and merged](https://github.com/drewstone/lw6-counterexample/pull/1); the published invocation now certifies the supplied coefficient triple.
The finite default certificates also replayed successfully.

The original constrained theorem is unaffected: this family keeps $I(C;B\mid A)>0$.
It disproves the proposed linear relaxation.
It does not settle the existence of every other possible unconstrained quantum entropy inequality.

The retained record includes an operator-supplied copy of correspondence reporting Andreas Winter's confirmation.
The original mailbox was not independently inspected in this review, and no peer-review or publication acceptance is asserted.
