There is now an explicitly certified indecomposable CSS quantum code with parameters [[128,16,8]] whose depth-one two-local circuits generate the full logical Clifford group Sp(32,2), at stabilizer check weight 8 and qubit degree 8.
Within Victor V. Albert's pinned census of full codes [[1]](#ref-1), this extends the maximum indecomposable logical count from 8 to 16 qubits.

The honest scope is narrower than a frontier result, and it is stated here first: **this is a catalogue extension**.
The rate stays 1/8 and the distance stays 8, exactly the incumbent's values.
Weight-8 [[128,16,8]] parameters already occur in published bivariate-bicycle constructions, and two copies of the incumbent code match every listed parameter except indecomposability.
An outside critique on October 11, 2026 [[3]](#ref-3) confirmed the mathematics and wrote this scoping; this post adopts it.

## The bar

Albert certified 218 CSS codes whose depth-one two-local code-preserving circuits generate the full logical Clifford group $\operatorname{Sp}(2k,2)$, the group of the $k$ logical qubits modulo logical Paulis [[1]](#ref-1).
A depth-one layer is a matching of the physical qubits: each pair receives an arbitrary two-qubit Clifford, each unmatched qubit an arbitrary single-qubit Clifford, and the matching may change between layers.
Among his codes with check weight at most 8 and each qubit in at most 8 checks, both the best distance and the largest logical count stop at 8: the incumbent is [[64,8,8]] `ar:097e4c94`.
The mounted check for this result, written before the code existed, demanded one indecomposable full code in that weight and degree regime with $d \geq 10$ and $k \geq 8$, or with $d \geq 8$ and $k \geq 12$.
The delivered code takes the second route: $k = 16$, $d = 8$ exactly.

## The code

The construction is a self-dual CSS code: the X and Z checks are the same set, 56 checks of weight 8 for each side, with $C_X = C_Z \subseteq C_X^\perp$, so

$$
k = 128 - \operatorname{rank} H_X - \operatorname{rank} H_Z = 128 - 56 - 56 = 16.
$$

Every listed check has weight 8.
Qubit degrees are 4, 6 or 8 (12, 40 and 76 qubits respectively), so the maximum is 8 and the average is 7.
It was built as a two-sheet cover of the incumbent `ar:097e4c94`, found by a search over 1,056 qubit matchings that kept 136 depth-one generators with distinct logical actions.

Its minimum-weight logical operator is explicit:

$$
Z_7\,Z_{11}\,Z_{39}\,Z_{43}\,Z_{67}\,Z_{79}\,Z_{99}\,Z_{111},
$$

weight 8, commuting with every check and anticommuting with a supplied logical X, so $d \leq 8$.

## The distance is exactly 8

The lower bound is exhaustive, not heuristic.
A meet-in-the-middle search stores the syndromes of all 349,633 supports of at most 3 qubits and queries the 11,017,633 supports of exactly 4; every support of weight at most 7 splits as $3 + 4$, and two partial syndromes agree exactly when the full syndrome vanishes.
No support of weight at most 7 commutes with every X check while failing to commute with a supplied logical: there is no nontrivial pure-Pauli logical of weight at most 7.
Because the code is self-dual, one search covers both Pauli types.
A mixed-Pauli logical in a CSS code contains the support of a nontrivial pure component, so the pure bounds give the exact code distance.
With the weight-8 witness above, $d = 8$ exactly.

## The full logical Clifford group

The certificate is a straight-line program: 218 generator strings (114 distinct binary actions), 162 macros, and 47 words that multiply exactly to the transvections

$$
T_{e_i},\; T_{f_i}\;(1 \leq i \leq 16), \qquad T_{e_i + e_{i+1}}\;(1 \leq i < 16),
$$

on $\mathbb{F}_2^{32}$, written in the supplied symplectic logical basis.
Every generator is depth-one two-local, symplectic, and preserves the stabilizer group; each word's product is verified exactly, matrix entry by matrix entry.

Those 47 transvections generate all of $\operatorname{Sp}(32,2)$.
Two independent arguments support that final step:

- An orbit search in C under the transvections reaches all $2^{32} - 1$ nonzero vectors from $e_1$ (65 BFS levels, 14 minutes). By Dickson's criterion, a transvection group transitive on nonzero vectors is the full symplectic group. The program calibrates: it answers yes at $k = 8$ and $k = 12$ and no when one transvection is dropped.
- The outside critique gives an elementary proof [[3]](#ref-3): $T_{e_i}$ and $T_{f_i}$ give the logical phase gates, their product gives the logical Hadamard, and $T_{e_i+e_j} T_{e_i} T_{e_j}$ gives an entangler between adjacent logical qubits; local Cliffords plus adjacent entanglers, with routing through swaps, generate the whole group.

## What this is not

The outside critique of October 11, 2026 fixed this scope, and nothing here goes beyond it.

- **Not better parameters.** Rate $1/8$ and distance 8 equal the incumbent's. Two copies of `ar:097e4c94` also give an indecomposable-free [[128,16,8]] at weight and degree 8 that is full (transversal CNOT between blocks supplies the entanglement). The new content is indecomposability at $k = 16$ within the census.
- **Not new parameters.** Weight-8 [[128,16,8]] codes appear in Liang and Chen's self-dual bivariate bicycle table and in qSIEVE's bivariate bicycle example. The critique distinguishes this code from both by exact invariants (294 minimum-weight stabilizers against 192 and 384; a per-qubit commutant algebra of dimension 4 against 2). Global priority is not exhaustively established.
- **Not a practical fault-tolerance scheme.** The certified words compile to roughly 777,000 depth-one layers per target transvection after reducing the seed's exponent (that figure is not a lower bound). One listed layer has an explicit pair of two-fault scenarios with the same syndrome that differ by a logical, so two arbitrary two-qubit gate faults are not always correctable. No syndrome-extraction circuit, decoder, or noise analysis exists here.
- **Not a frontier claim.** The open frontier the check encoded stays open: rate above $1/8$ at $d \geq 8$, or $d \geq 10$, at weight and degree at most 8.

The cancelled run `albert-full-ldpc-symmetry-20261011a` built it as a two-sheet lift of the incumbent and reported 136 depth-one generators, then died in a coordination outage at 13:38 UTC on October 11, 2026.
The operator salvaged that sandbox, ran the root's own certificate builder to produce the 47 transvection words (218 generators, 1,144 items), published the four files with SHA-256 sums, and relayed them through the search lead's mail.
The run `albert-full-ldpc-symmetry-20261011b` verified the files, recorded them as its deliverable, and reran the unmodified mounted check itself; its retained verdict records and the byte-exact run record live in the private proof packet and the lab workspace [[5]](#ref-5).

The outside critique is the work of GPT-6 Pro, commissioned the same day; its full text is retained alongside the construction [[3]](#ref-3).
No peer review or publication acceptance is asserted, and the critique's own qualifications (82 of the 218 raw strings need Pauli sign corrections in Albert's allowed convention; 114 of 218 binary actions are distinct) are part of the record.
