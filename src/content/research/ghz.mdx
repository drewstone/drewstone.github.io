## The state

Take the four-qubit Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger state

$$
|\mathrm{GHZ}_4\rangle=\frac{|0000\rangle+|1111\rangle}{\sqrt2}
$$

and define

$$
\rho=\frac34|\mathrm{GHZ}_4\rangle\!\langle\mathrm{GHZ}_4|
+\frac18|1010\rangle\!\langle1010|
+\frac18|1001\rangle\!\langle1001|.
$$

The [BCWW candidate inequality](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.05650) uses only proper subsystems.
Tracing out any nonempty complement kills the GHZ off-diagonal term.
Consequently, every entropy used in that inequality equals the Shannon entropy of the corresponding marginal of the classical distribution

$$
P(0000)=P(1111)=\frac38,\qquad
P(1010)=P(1001)=\frac18.
$$

The quantum construction therefore has a simple classical verification.

## The exact violation

Using the [functional defined on the classical-witness page](/research/bcww#the-statement), substitution gives

$$
F=\frac{25}{4}-\frac98\log_2 3-\frac{15}{8}\log_2 5
 =0.113302008774\ldots>0.
$$

Multiplying by eight reduces its positive sign to

$$
2^{50}>3^9 5^{15}.
$$

Both sides are integers:

$$
1125899906842624>600677490234375.
$$

The retained standard-library verifier reconstructs every required marginal and compares the result with the closed form.
It was replayed successfully during the October 2 evidence review.

## What the construction establishes

This is a second witness against the same proposed BCWW inequality.
Its relevant entropy vector is also realized classically.
The violation does not show an advantage that requires quantum coherence.

The retained provenance note attributes this construction to the fifth arm of an operator-led verification fleet dated August 10, 2026.
That note records five independent verification approaches, including blind arithmetic and fresh code.
The complete original fleet transcripts were not recovered in this review, so the note supports an attribution claim rather than a fully reconstructed discovery trajectory.
