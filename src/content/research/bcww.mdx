## The statement

Bao, Cao, Walter, and Wang proposed a candidate inequality for general four-party quantum states in [equation (4.6) of their 2015 paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.05650).
Writing $S(X)$ for the entropy of subsystem $X$, its left side is

$$
\begin{aligned}
F={}&S(ABD)+S(ABC)+S(BCD)\\
&-2S(BD)-2S(BC)+S(CD)\\
&-S(AD)-S(AC)-S(AB)+2S(B)+S(A).
\end{aligned}
$$

The proposed bound is $F\leq 0$.
The equation is numbered (4.16) in the paper's first version.
This result concerns that candidate inequality, not the paper's full set of holographic entropy results.

## The witness

Let $(A,B,C,D)$ be uniformly distributed on

$$
\{0001,\;0010,\;0011,\;0111,\;1000\}.
$$

Each atom has probability $1/5$.
The classical distribution defines a diagonal quantum state, so a classical violation also disproves the proposed general quantum bound.
All logarithms on this page use base two.

The nonzero marginal multiplicities are enough to calculate every term:

| Subsystem | Multiplicities out of five |
| --- | --- |
| $A$, $B$ | $4,1$ |
| $AB$, $AC$, $AD$ | $3,1,1$ |
| $BC$, $BD$ | $2,2,1$ |
| $CD$ | $2,1,1,1$ |
| $ABC$, $ABD$ | $2,1,1,1$ |
| $BCD$ | $1,1,1,1,1$ |

Substituting $H=\log_2 5-\frac15\sum_j n_j\log_2 n_j$ gives

$$
F=\frac{9\log_2 3-14}{5}
 =0.052932501298\ldots>0.
$$

The sign has an elementary integer certificate:

$$
3^9=19683>16384=2^{14}.
$$

## Verification and correction

The [public verification repository](https://github.com/drewstone/bcww-counterexample) contains the witness, a numerical checker, and Lean proof material.
The integer comparison above establishes the positive sign independently of floating point.
A numerical browser calculation is an illustration of that proof.

An earlier archived smoke run named $\{0000,0010,0100,1000,1101\}$ as its witness.
That atom set does **not** establish the advertised violation.
A later verification pursuit recorded the inconsistency on September 3, 2026.
The corrected atoms are the ones printed on this page; the interactive view lets you compare both sets.
